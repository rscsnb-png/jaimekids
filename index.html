About us

Every child deserves to feel loved, valued and remembered.

J'aimeKids was founded by Chérie Schutte (formerly Chérie Theron) in March 2007. Since then, it has touched thousands of children's lives through community outreach, therapeutic assistance, child advocacy, educational support and charitable initiatives.

We support vulnerable, disadvantaged, abused and abandoned children, children with disabilities and children going through difficult life circumstances. Whatever their situation, we believe every child deserves to feel loved, valued, supported and remembered.

The meaning behind our name

J'aime + Kids

“J'aime” is French for “I love”. Put together, our name simply says “I love kids”, and that is the heart of everything we do.

Our mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children through meaningful emotional, developmental, educational and practical support, while creating opportunities for children to experience the joy of childhood.

Our vision

A South Africa in which every child has the opportunity to experience hope, happiness, safety, belonging and access to the support they need to reach their potential.

Our core values

  • Compassion
  • Child dignity
  • Inclusion
  • Community involvement
  • Child protection
  • Empowerment
  • Integrity and transparency
  • Meaningful and sustainable support
  • Creating moments of childhood joy

Our relationship with Aldeia

J'aimeKids is the registered non-profit organisation that heads Aldeia. Through Aldeia's network of therapeutic learning centres, practitioners and communities across South Africa, we can identify children who need support, coordinate outreach, mobilise volunteers and run charitable initiatives in several provinces. J'aimeKids remains an independent charity with its own identity and purpose.

Our projects

Support beyond the gift

Children facing trauma, neglect or hardship need more than material help. Our work has always combined practical support with emotional, developmental and educational care.

Active · 2026

Christmas outreach

Our signature Christmas truck drives once took decorated, light-covered trucks through communities like Jan Hofmeyr and Danville in Pretoria. In 2026 the campaign returns nationally, reaching 350 children in four provinces.

Active

Educational sponsorship

We sponsor financially disadvantaged children who need therapeutic learning, developmental and behavioural support through the Aldeia network.

Past project

Hospital visits

Visits to paediatric wards, including Leratong Hospital, brought gifts, teddy bears, companionship and Christmas celebrations to children receiving treatment.

Past project

Children's homes

Christmas celebrations, birthday parties, recreational activities and educational projects for children living in children's homes and other care facilities.

Past project

Child advocacy and protection

Victim empowerment work prepared and supported children through court proceedings to reduce secondary trauma, alongside child abuse awareness and community education.

Past project

Therapeutic support

Working with Child Welfare, we funded therapists who provided professional help to vulnerable children across South Africa.

Past project

Disability inclusion

With the Muscular Dystrophy community, we organised therapeutic horseback riding so children with disabilities could ride and enjoy horses in a supported setting.

Past project

Community outreach

Outreach in informal settlements and vulnerable areas offered community education, emotional support and help for children facing homelessness or difficult home circumstances.

Our story and impact

  1. 2007

    J'aimeKids is born

    Chérie Theron (now Schutte) founds J'aimeKids in March to support abused, abandoned and vulnerable children.

  2. 2010

    “Changing the world one child at a time”

    The Krugersdorp News introduces J'aimeKids and its outreach and child abuse awareness programmes (17 September 2010).

  3. 2012

    The Christmas truck rolls out

    In December, a truck carrying around 500 gifts leaves the Live It congregation at NG Kerk Columba, with transport from Imperial Truck Hire. Looklocal Roodepoort covers it as “Vragmotor van Blydskap”.

  4. 2013

    Therapeutic horseback riding

    On 22 June, children affected by muscular dystrophy ride horses in a supported environment. The day is featured in the Muscular Dystrophy Foundation magazine.

  5. Today

    Working through the Aldeia network

    J'aimeKids heads Aldeia and sponsors children's therapeutic learning and developmental support through its centres.

  6. 2026

    Christmas goes national

    Every Child Deserves Christmas aims to reach 350 children across Mpumalanga, Gauteng, the Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal.

Privacy policy (POPIA)

J'aimeKids respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect: only the details you give us through our enquiry form or by phone, such as your name, contact details, province and message.

Why we collect it: to respond to your enquiry, coordinate volunteering, sponsorships and donations, and issue Section 18A certificates where you request them.

How messages reach us: messages sent through our contact form are delivered to our inbox by email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service. We do not use them for marketing.

How we protect it: we keep your information secure, share it only with people who need it to help you (for example the Aldeia branch in your province), and never sell it.

Online donations: donations are processed by BackaBuddy. Your payment details are handled under BackaBuddy's own privacy policy and are never seen or stored by J'aimeKids.

Your rights: you may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to its use, by calling 010 006 5199. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children's photographs: we only publish photographs of children with appropriate consent and in a way that respects their dignity. If you would like an image removed, please contact us.
